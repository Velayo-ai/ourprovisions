Changing his mind, three ways, on both screens: tap the tile again, tap the item in the cart, or tap ↶ to take the last one out. If the cart is full, a new tile makes it shake: take one out first. Nothing lands until That's it! (✓ on Simple). After that, changing a pick is a grown-up job, done on the list.

The cart: line work in espresso, like the Velayo horizon mark: a sand sun arc on the basket, a BVI-teal waterline and two ripples. The items sit inside, behind the front panel. The celebration: his own picks roll in, in the cart, while the sun comes up over the water. No score, no sound, no prices, no other names. With reduced motion, everything just appears.
