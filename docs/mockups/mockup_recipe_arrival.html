What this mockup decides

  • One arrival sheet for every recipe that comes from outside the library — screenshot, pasted text, a share code, a Home notice (later: email, and the Galley folds in).
  • One verb: Add to our meals. By the Add/New rule, an arriving recipe is brought in, not created. The decline is Not for us, never a destructive word.
  • Three ingredient states: matched (plain), New item (not in your catalog until you add), Check this (the reader wasn't sure — never guessed silently).
  • No teal anywhere on these screens: nothing here is a completion. The add itself gets the ordinary toast.
  • Open for the specs: the eyebrow wording for pasted text (“From what you pasted”?); the Home notice's lifetime; whether the share note is editable by the recipient (lean: no, it's Andrew's words).
