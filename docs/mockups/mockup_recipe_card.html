Sheet-Pan Salmon

Ingredients
Salmon fillets
6
Asparagus, trimmed
2 bunches
Lemon
2
Olive oil
3 tbsp
Garlic cloves
4
Instructions
Heat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
Toss asparagus with half the oil, half the garlic, salt and pepper. Spread on the pan.
Nestle salmon among the asparagus. Rub with remaining oil and garlic, top with lemon slices.
Roast 12–14 minutes, until salmon flakes easily.
Notes
From the original Swap asparagus for green beans in winter. Leftover salmon is great flaked into a salad the next day.
