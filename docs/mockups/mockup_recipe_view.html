Apple Pie

from Grandma Phyllis
Added by Elly
Dessert · 9 ingredients · 7 steps
+ Add to This Week
Make it
Cook modeBigger steps, screen stays on
Start

Ingredients 9

  • 6 cups sliced apples
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 pie crusts

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss the apples with both sugars, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg and lemon juice.
  3. Line the pie dish with one crust and fill with the apples.
  4. Dot with butter, top with the second crust, crimp and cut vents.
  5. Bake 40–45 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  6. Cool at least 2 hours before slicing.
  7. Serve with vanilla ice cream.
